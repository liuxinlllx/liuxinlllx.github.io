## About This Episode

"Exercise is Medicine" is repeatedly mentioned in policy documents — but how far has it actually progressed in practice? In this episode, I draw on my own research experience to discuss the origins and evolution of this concept, as well as innovative practices emerging across China.

## Key Topics

### Policy Timeline
- 2016: The *"Healthy China 2030" Planning Outline* first incorporated "sport-medicine integration" into a national-level policy document
- 2021: The *National Fitness Plan (2021–2025)* further emphasized "Exercise is Medicine"
- In the upcoming 15th Five-Year Plan, Exercise is Medicine is expected to play an even more prominent role

### Local Practice Cases
Zhejiang, Shanghai, Shenzhen, and other regions have begun piloting models that combine "exercise prescription" with community health management. Zhejiang's approach is particularly noteworthy — community health centers issue exercise prescriptions, and certified social sports instructors follow up on their implementation.

### Core Challenges
- Doctors don't understand exercise, and sports professionals don't understand medicine — how do we break down these professional silos?
- The payment question: who pays for exercise prescriptions? Can health insurance cover them?
- Standardization: what counts as a "qualified" exercise prescription?

### My Reflections
Exercise is Medicine is not purely a sports issue — it is a social governance challenge. It requires cross-departmental coordination among health commissions, sports bureaus, and education departments. The researcher's mission is to provide quantifiable evidence that can drive policy innovation.
